# ADR 0002: Allow AI-assisted development

## Status

Accepted

## Context

OrgTeX is being developed with the assistance of modern development tools,
including AI systems.

AI-assisted development can improve productivity, documentation, debugging and
design exploration.

However, generated output may contain mistakes, unsuitable designs or material
whose provenance requires additional review.

The project therefore needs a clear policy regarding AI-assisted contributions.

## Decision

OrgTeX allows AI-assisted development.

AI-generated or AI-assisted contributions must be reviewed, understood and
validated by a human contributor before being accepted into the project.

The contributor remains responsible for the contribution.

AI assistance does not override licensing, attribution, security, testing or
quality requirements.

## Consequences

Contributors may use AI tools without making OrgTeX cease to be free and
open-source software.

AI-assisted contributions are held to the same standards as other
contributions.

Potential third-party licensing issues must still be reviewed independently.

The project should favor code that human maintainers can understand and
maintain.
